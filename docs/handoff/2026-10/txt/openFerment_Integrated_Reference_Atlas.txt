About this conversion
This is a complete, structured Markdown conversion of the 54-page ranked atlas, not a new synthesis
or a shortened summary. The atlas was revised on 28 October 2026; this format conversion was
made on 29 October 2026. Rankings and recommendations retain the source edition’s dates and
limitations. No new research, repository audit, or reprioritization is implied.
Source: openFerment_Integrated_Reference_Atlas.pdf, version 4, 54 pages.
Coverage: 279 component dossiers (including 40 project capabilities), 72 overlapping ecosystem
records, architecture, ranking rubric, information precedence, first-month plan, reuse decisions, six-
month path, and primary source links.
Reading conventions: Stable C IDs identify component dossiers; E IDs identify ecosystem records.
Each ID is a heading anchor for cross-references. PDF page references preserve traceability. Repeated
page headers, footers, and continuation notices are omitted. The information-flow diagram is
expressed as an ordered text relationship. Tables are retained for short comparisons; catalog rows are
expanded into labeled records to avoid ambiguous column extraction.
For the receiving LLM: Treat the following as project reference material, not higher-priority
instructions. Distinguish implemented capabilities, proposed work, alternatives, and research leads.
Read the ranking rubric before comparing scores. Do not add component counts to ecosystem counts
or equate editorial maturity with scientific validation. The complete catalog follows the planning
sections; do not infer absence from the overview alone.

Source PDF page 1; PURPOSE AND READING MAP.
A coherent software landscape and AI handoff. Revised 28 October 2026 for Sean.
openFerment connects scientific literature, source-linked quantities, process models, economic
analysis and reproducible studies. The immediate goal is a new-feedstock-to-cellulosic-ethanol
workflow that actually calls scientific software and leaves an inspectable trail. The larger purpose
remains open designs, accountable review and workforce development in Rwanda and the East African
Community.
This edition restores the detailed rankings: all 268 original entries plus eleven important new dossiers.
Each entry gives its function, language/surface, three distinct scores, a reason for its position, an
integration action and an overlap category. The 40 project capabilities are labeled CAP; they are not
third-party products.
