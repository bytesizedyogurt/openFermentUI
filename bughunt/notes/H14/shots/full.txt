A fit takes only measurements; a simulation names every source

mu_max, Xmax and the production and uptake coefficients of a fit are fitted to a real fermentation in a specific vessel, from a deposition and nothing else. A simulation may take a parameter from a paper’s records, a fit, a kept artifact, a person’s assumption or a stated convention, and it names which for every one: a number lifted from a paper about another strain in another reactor is labelled as that, and the simulation’s provenance follows the weakest of its sources. It is never a measurement.

Titre is fermOS's output — and the seam on either side

Upstream, geneOS is stoichiometry: a genome-scale model gives a yield ceiling with no time axis. fermOS is dynamics: the curve in a specific vessel. The gap between the ceiling and the titre actually achieved is exactly what reconciliation measures.

Downstream, the seam to pureOS is the harvest step, and it carries a decision made back in geneOS: secreted or intracellular. A secreted product starts at centrifuge and filter; an intracellular one starts at lysis and inherits every problem after it. The handoff carries the product’s location beside its titre.

The train starts with a decision made in geneOS

Secreted or intracellular is the single choice with the largest effect on downstream cost, and it is a genetic design choice — made in geneOS when the chassis and signal peptide are picked, long before anybody touches a column. A secreted product starts at centrifuge and filter. An intracellular one starts at lysis and inherits every problem after it. So the handoff from fermOS carries the product’s location beside its titre.

Properties flow downstream and are stored once. Thermostability is predicted in geneOS and consumed here, where it decides whether a heat step can replace most of the capture chromatography. It stays a geneOS field, so one question keeps one answer.

ethanol_glucose_v0
Glucose to ethanol, draft v0

From this repository

A glucose solution into a batch fermenter, then the ethanol purification train of the BioSTEAM reference designs (beer column, rectifier, molecular sieves), giving fuel-grade ethanol. The fermenter turns glucose into ethanol at the bound yield as a share of the theoretical yield, for a batch time of titre over productivity. Chemicals and finance are the dry-grind corn ethanol plant's; equipment and thermodynamics are BioSTEAM's own; no number is the flowsheet's.

From this repository
