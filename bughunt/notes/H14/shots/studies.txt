ethanol_glucose_v0
Glucose to ethanol, draft v0

From this repository

A glucose solution into a batch fermenter, then the ethanol purification train of the BioSTEAM reference designs (beer column, rectifier, molecular sieves), giving fuel-grade ethanol. The fermenter turns glucose into ethanol at the bound yield as a share of the theoretical yield, for a batch time of titre over productivity. Chemicals and finance are the dry-grind corn ethanol plant's; equipment and thermodynamics are BioSTEAM's own; no number is the flowsheet's.

INPUTS
glucose in the fermenter's feed (g L-1): binds to records of medium_component_conc
ethanol titre at harvest (g L-1): binds to records of ethanol_titre
ethanol yield on glucose (g g-1): binds to records of ethanol_yield_substrate
ethanol volumetric productivity (g L-1 h-1): binds to records of ethanol_productivity
ethanol fermented in a year (t yr-1): a named person's assumption
price of glucose (USD kg-1): a named person's assumption
ethanol purity: a convention the run states

The price is USD per kg of fuel-grade ethanol, as the product leaves its tank.

custom_train_v1
A vessel and a train, priced

From this repository

A glucose solution into the fermenter fermOS designed (a kept fermos.vessel design: BioSTEAM's AeratedBioreactor or NREL batch fermenter, rebuilt as the same class with every design parameter as the design holds it, running the reaction the design was built on), its beer through the units of a train pureOS ran (a kept pureos.train_run: each unit the same catalogue type, class and values as that run held them), priced with the dry-grind corn ethanol plant's TEA (biorefineries.corn.create_tea, ConventionalEthanolTEA), its financial parameters in the panel's Finance group as the package's defaults until a person sets one. A scenario on it names the vessel design and the train run. The train's own inlet is not an input: the fermenter's beer is its inlet. Only the yeast program has a chemical set, so a vessel or a train of another program is refused.

INPUTS
glucose in the fermenter's feed (g L-1): binds to records of medium_component_conc
ethanol titre at harvest (g L-1): binds to records of ethanol_titre
ethanol yield on glucose (g g-1): binds to records of ethanol_yield_substrate
ethanol volumetric productivity (g L-1 h-1): binds to records of ethanol_productivity
ethanol fermented in a year (t yr-1): a named person's assumption
price of glucose (USD kg-1): a named person's assumption

The price is USD per kg of the product the train traces (ethanol, for the yeast program), contained in the train's product stream as it leaves the train's last unit.
