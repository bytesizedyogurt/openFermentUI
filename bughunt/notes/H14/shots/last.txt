Where geneOS stops and fermOS starts

geneOS is stoichiometry: what the engineered cell can do, at steady state, with no time axis and no vessel. A genome-scale model gives a yield ceiling. What a real fermenter achieves over ninety hours is a claim of another discipline, and it belongs to fermOS.

Titre is fermOS’s output. The gap between the ceiling here and the titre achieved there is what reconciliation measures against a deposition, so every number on this screen reads as a ceiling of the cell, and a vessel’s output is fermOS’s to predict.
